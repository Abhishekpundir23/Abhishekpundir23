# Technology icon attribution

Artwork source: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), pinned commit [`777807a262bb7384ff406fd4b35fdcd02e9514c3`](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/commit/777807a262bb7384ff406fd4b35fdcd02e9514c3).

The vendored SVG files are unchanged copies of icons from that commit. The profile generator embeds only their path geometry, scales them, and recolors them to match an accessible category palette. It does not fetch assets at build time or page-view time.

Its original [disclaimer](DISCLAIMER.simple-icons.md) and relevant upstream [brand metadata](source-metadata.json) are retained here. Brand marks belong to their respective owners; their display identifies technologies and does not imply endorsement.

Some original artwork has its own license, recorded by Simple Icons. Those declarations are preserved below; CC0 should not be read as overriding them. The Git logo is by Jason Long and is used under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/); the displayed version is scaled and recolored. The JavaScript logo source is the logo.js project, which Simple Icons identifies as MIT licensed.
